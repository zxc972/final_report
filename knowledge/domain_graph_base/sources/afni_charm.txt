<section id="overview">
<h2><a class="toc-backref" href="#id4" role="doc-backlink">Overview</a><a class="headerlink" href="#overview" title="Permalink to this heading">¶</a></h2>
<p>Here we present the Cortical Hierarchy Atlas of the Rhesus Macaque
(CHARM).</p>
<div class="line-block">
<div class="line">This atlas is defined in the coordinates/space of the NMT v2
template, described here:</div>
<div class="line"><a class="reference internal" href="template_nmtv2.html#nh-macaque-template-nmtv2"><span class="std std-ref">Macaque template:  NMT v2 (current)</span></a>.</div>
</div>
</section>
<section id="id1">
<h2><a class="toc-backref" href="#id5" role="doc-backlink">Contents</a><a class="headerlink" href="#id1" title="Permalink to this heading">¶</a></h2>
<p>The CHARM parcellates the macaque cortex at six spatial scales. The
finest level is based on the D99 atlas, while the broadest level forms
four cortical lobes. Between these levels, regions are progressively
grouped to form increasingly large composite structures. This allows
the researcher to choose the appropriate scale of investigation for
their study design and data.</p>
<table class="docutils align-default" id="id2">
<caption><span class="caption-text">Example of the CHARM hierarchical structure</span><a class="headerlink" href="#id2" title="Permalink to this table">¶</a></caption>

</thead>

</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>The CHARM is distributed as a single data set with six
sub-volumes. Note that for the purpose of using AFNI sub-brick
selectors, CHARM Level 1 is volume <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;[0]&quot;</span></code> and Level 6 is volume
<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">&quot;[5]&quot;</span></code>, because AFNI (like Python and C) use zero-base counting.</p>
<p>The CHARM is distributed with the NMT v2. The NMT v2 has several forms
and variants (based on symmetry/asymmetry, FOV and voxel size), and
for each of these there is an accompanying CHARM.</p>
<p>Note that the NMT download also includes a CSV table file of the ROI
labels, hierarchically organized.  This can be viewed as a simple text
file or within any spreadsheet software, such as shown in the
following image.</p>

<section id="download">
<h2><a class="toc-backref" href="#id7" role="doc-backlink">Download</a><a class="headerlink" href="#download" title="Permalink to this heading">¶</a></h2>
<p>The CHARM is distributed as part of the NMT v2 download. For each
form/variant of the NMT, there is a matching CHARM dataset, as well.</p>
<p>To download and unpack the datasets, please see the instructions...</p>
<ul class="simple">
<li><p>... here for the <strong>symmetric</strong> form:
<a class="reference internal" href="template_nmtv2.html#nh-macaque-template-nmtv2-sym-dl"><span class="std std-ref">Download symmetric NMT v2 datasets</span></a></p></li>
<li><p>... here for the <strong>asymmetric</strong> form:
<a class="reference internal" href="template_nmtv2.html#nh-macaque-template-nmtv2-asym-dl"><span class="std std-ref">Download asymmetric NMT v2 datasets</span></a></p></li>
</ul>
</section>
